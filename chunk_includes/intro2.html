1. Some dashes will appear on the screen and you press the space bar to see each new word of the sentence.

2. Read at a natural rate, comprehending what you read.

3. You'll see a question about the sentence and should answer by pressing the "F" key for YES or the "J" key for NO. You will be told if your answer was incorrect. If so, you should take this as an indication to read more slowly and carefully.

4. After the question, the computer will automatically go on to the next sentence.
