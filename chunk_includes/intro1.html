Please maximize your browser window before beginning!

Welcome. In this experiment, you'll be reading some sentences on the computer screen. You will first see 1 or 2 rows of dashes like this:

--- ----- ------ --- ---- --------- --- --- ----- ------- --

--- --------.

These dashes indicate where the words of the sentence will appear. With every press of the space bar, a new word will appear and the previous word will become dashes again.

Please read at a comfortable, natural pace, and make sure that you understand what you read. Don't read so fast that you don't fully understand the sentence, but don't read so slow that you forget parts of the sentence that you've already read.

When you finish reading the last word, press the space bar again. The dashes will go away and you will see a question about the sentence you just read. To answer the question, press the "F" key for YES or the "J" key for NO. You will be reminded which key is "yes" and which is "no".

Accuracy is important, so please take this seriously.

If you are unsure of the answer (or think that both answers are right), try to pick the best possible answer.

To be able to read and answer efficiently, you should keep your fingers resting on the space bar and the "F" and "J" keys, so that you don't have to look down at the keys to answer. You can use whichever hand is most comfortable for you to press the keys, or use both hands if you like.
